# wiki/

This is **Claude's** domain — pages created and maintained by Claude, synthesized from the material in `../raw/`.

- `index.md` — master catalog of every page in the wiki. Kept up to date whenever a page is added, moved, or removed.
- `log.md` — append-only log of activity (what was added, when, and why). Never edited retroactively, only appended to.
- `concepts/` — pages about ideas, topics, and subject-matter knowledge.
- `projects/` — pages tracking specific projects and their state.
- `people/` — dossiers on individuals (colleagues, collaborators, contacts).

Unlike `raw/`, pages here are living documents: they get rewritten, restructured, and cross-linked over time as understanding improves.
